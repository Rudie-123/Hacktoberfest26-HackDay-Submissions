# What we built
Problem:
Students often struggle to find hackathons and technical opportunities because information is scattered across multiple platforms. They also find it difficult to identify suitable courses based on their skills, interests, budget, and career goals. Furthermore, after participating in hackathons, students often lack personalized feedback to understand their mistakes and identify areas for improvement.

Solution:
Opportunity Atlas is a unified platform that brings hackathon discovery, course recommendations, and AI-powered learning together. It allows students to explore hackathons based on dates, location, cost, difficulty, and effort required; discover suitable courses and personalized learning paths; and use an AI chatbot to analyze their hackathon projects, identify potential weaknesses, and receive actionable improvement plans. By connecting learning with real-world opportunities, the platform helps students continuously develop their skills and prepare for future challenges.

# Limitations
1. Data availability: Hackathon and course information comes from multiple providers and may not always be updated immediately.

2. Accuracy of difficulty and effort ratings: Estimated ratings may differ from the actual experience of a participant. Organizer-provided ratings should be distinguished from platform estimates.

3. AI limitations: The AI Hackathon Coach may misunderstand project context or produce incorrect suggestions. Its feedback is most useful when the student provides a clear problem statement, project evidence and judging criteria.

4. Course access and pricing: Some courses provide free learning materials but charge for certificates or graded assessments. Prices and access conditions may change.

5. External integrations: Automated listings, document analysis, reminders and recommendations may require API access, database configuration and additional development.

6. No guaranteed outcomes: Completing recommended courses or following AI suggestions does not guarantee hackathon selection, prizes, internships or employment.

7. Initial coverage: A first version may support only selected domains, providers or regions.


# How to Use Opportunity Atlas

1. Open the Opportunity Atlas website.
2. Sign up or log in to your account.
3. Create your profile by entering your skills and interests.
4. Select your preferred technology domain, such as AI, web development or cybersecurity.
5. Navigate to the Hackathon Finder section.
6. Search for hackathons based on your interests.
7. Filter hackathons by difficulty level, effort level, location and date.
8. Check whether registration is free or paid.
9. Review eligibility criteria, required skills and registration deadlines.
10. Save interesting hackathons and open their official registration links.
11. Navigate to the Course Finder section.
12. Select the skill you want to learn and your current experience level.
13. Specify your budget, preferred duration and learning goals.
14. Explore recommended courses from different learning platforms.
15. Choose a suitable course and follow its enrollment link.
16. Follow your personalized learning roadmap and practise through projects.
17. Participate in a hackathon and save your project details.
18. Open the AI Hackathon Coach and upload your project report, presentation and available judges' feedback.
19. Ask the AI chatbot to identify weaknesses, suggest improvements and recommend relevant courses.
20. Track your progress through your dashboard and use the recommendations to prepare for future hackathons.

# Conclusion

Opportunity Atlas brings together opportunity discovery, skill development and performance improvement in a single platform. Its main advantage is the connection between hackathons, recommended learning resources and AI-generated feedback. Instead of simply helping students find opportunities, it aims to help them prepare, reflect on their experiences and continuously improve their skills.

The long-term goal is to create a personalized learning and opportunity ecosystem that helps students turn their technical interests into demonstrable skills and real-world experience.
